# Elseyworks style guide

Lynn's rules for how the website looks and reads. Claude applies these to every page and article,
and adds a new line here whenever Lynn gives a new rule.

## Headlines
- Every headline uses one font and one colour for the whole headline (no mixed styles or colours within it).
- No full stop at the end of a headline.

## Look
- The site is always light (cream background, dark text), whatever the visitor's computer is set to. No dark version.

## Pictures
- The photo of Lynn at the top of the home page is square (not arch-shaped).
- The name "Elseyworks" (top of the page and footer) is all in one font, a plain, easy-to-read italic serif (no flowery letters) throughout: "Elsey" in black, "works" in the rust colour (not bold).
- The top menu ends with "About Lynn" and then a search box with a magnifying-glass icon (far right). There are no "Read the writing" / "About Lynn" buttons on the home page.
