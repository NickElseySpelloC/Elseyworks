# Elseyworks style guide

Lynn's rules for how the website looks and reads. Claude applies these to every page and article,
and adds a new line here whenever Lynn gives a new rule.

## Headlines
- Every headline uses one font and one colour for the whole headline (no mixed styles or colours within it).
- No full stop at the end of a headline.

## Look
- The site is always light (cream background, dark text), whatever the visitor's computer is set to. No dark version.
